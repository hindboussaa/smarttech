How to Back Up Phone Photos Before Changing Phones

Published October 2026 · About 4 minutes to read

A checklist for moving precious pictures without accidental deletion. This guide explains what to check first, what to avoid, and when to ask for help.

1. Choose a backup destination

You can use a trusted cloud service, a computer, or an external drive. Ideally keep two copies in different places, especially for irreplaceable photos.

2. Check available space

Make sure the destination has enough storage for both photos and videos. Large video files can use more space than expected.

3. Turn on backup carefully

On iPhone, review iCloud Photos settings; on Android, review your photo app’s backup settings. Follow the provider’s on-screen steps and allow time for uploads on Wi-Fi.

4. Verify actual files

Open your backup from another device or browser and check several recent and older photos, including videos. A progress icon or “backup on” switch alone is not proof that every item arrived.

5. Transfer to the new phone

Sign in to the correct account and follow the new phone’s transfer assistant. Keep the old phone powered on until you have checked albums, contacts and important app data.

6. Do not wipe the old device too soon

Wait until you can open your photos on the new phone and in your independent backup. Remember that deleting synced photos on one device may remove them from the cloud and other devices.

Before you finish

For especially important pictures, keep an extra offline copy on an external drive stored safely.

Settings and features vary by device, software version and region. When in doubt, use the official help pages for your device or service.
