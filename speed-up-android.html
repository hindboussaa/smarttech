How to Make Your Android Phone Feel Faster

Updated October 2026 · Estimated reading time: 3 minutes

If your Android phone feels slower than it used to, start with simple checks before installing any “cleaner” apps. Menu names vary between manufacturers.

1. Restart your phone

Restarting closes stuck processes and can clear temporary glitches. Hold the power button and choose Restart if available.

2. Check free storage

Open Settings and look for Storage. Delete downloads you no longer need, move photos to a trusted backup, and uninstall apps you don't use. Confirm files are backed up before deleting them.

3. Update Android and your apps

Go to Settings and search for Software update. Open the Play Store to check for app updates. Updates may fix bugs and improve stability.

4. Review battery and background activity

Settings usually includes a Battery section showing which apps use power. If an app consumes an unusual amount, check its settings or uninstall it if unnecessary.

5. Avoid risky “speed booster” promises

Third-party booster apps may add ads, collect data, or run continuously. Built-in storage and app management tools are generally a better starting point.

When to get help

If the phone overheats, restarts repeatedly, or the battery swells, stop troubleshooting and contact a qualified repair provider.
