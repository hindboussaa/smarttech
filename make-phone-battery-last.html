How to Make Your Phone Battery Last Longer During the Day

Published October 2026 · About 4 minutes to read

Settings and habits that help when your phone struggles to reach evening. This guide explains what to check first, what to avoid, and when to ask for help.

1. Check what uses power

Open your phone’s Battery settings and inspect usage by app. A navigation app, video call or weak signal may explain a sudden drop.

2. Lower screen brightness

Use adaptive brightness or reduce it manually. Shorten the screen timeout so the display does not stay on unnecessarily.

3. Use battery-saving mode

Turn on Low Power Mode on iPhone or Battery Saver on Android when needed. Some background activity and notifications may be delayed.

4. Review background activity

Check which apps refresh or use location in the background. Change permissions for apps that do not need constant access; keep important safety and navigation functions working.

5. Keep the phone comfortable

Avoid leaving your phone in a hot car or direct sun. High heat can accelerate battery ageing and may cause temporary performance limits.

6. Update apps and system software

Install updates from official stores and system settings. If a battery problem began after an update, monitor usage for a few days and check the manufacturer’s support information.

7. Consider battery health

Older batteries naturally hold less charge. Check battery health information if your model provides it; if capacity is very reduced, professional battery replacement may help.

Before you finish

Battery life varies with device age, signal strength, screen usage and temperature; no single setting guarantees a specific number of hours.

Settings and features vary by device, software version and region. When in doubt, use the official help pages for your device or service.
